Healthcare Call Center Performance Analysis

Healthcare call centers play an important role in connecting patients with information and services, making timely call handling an important operational concern. This project integrates and analyzes three healthcare call-center datasets covering 9.6 million calls across California counties to evaluate call volume, wait times, and caller abandonment.

Using SQL, Python, and Power BI, the analysis examines how wait times relate to caller abandonment, identifies counties experiencing the greatest combination of call demand and performance challenges, and evaluates changes in call-center performance over time. The analysis found a 0.603 correlation between wait time and caller abandonment, with abandonment increasing from 6.25% at 15-minute waits to 34.69% at waits of 60 minutes or more.

The project concludes by identifying Tulare, Los Angeles, Kern, and Sacramento as priority counties based on high call volumes, long wait times, and elevated abandonment rates, and recommends targeted staffing and alternative service channels to help reduce queue pressure, wait times, and caller abandonment.
